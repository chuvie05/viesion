Pink Threat

A digital illustration exploring the dark side of toxic parasocial relationships between fans and idols. Combining 2D illustration, vector, and 3D modelling in Blender, the piece uses bold colour contrast and unsettling imagery to show both perspectives — the idol's confidence and the obsessive reach of the hands around her.
